# Planner and book facts

## Planner (`TaRL_Math_SLOs_and_33Day_Planner_28Oct.pdf`)

Rules that reached Day 1 (28 Oct re-issue):

- **M-B-01** "Count objects up to 20 and say how many" runs **Days 1 and 2 only**: Day 1 Teach and model, Day 2 Practise and check. No guided-practice day.
- Difficulty line for M-B-01: "counting 1-20 with objects in rows or small groups; avoid numbers above 20". Enforced in code: no number above 20; scatter layouts only for 9 or fewer objects.
- **M-B-02** (count, read and write to 100) starts **Day 3**. Writing numerals starts Day 3, not Day 1, so Day 1 is counting only (do not print "Counting only today").
- Tens and ones (**M-B-06**) is folded into **Day 12**.
- Every SLO has a **"Builds on"** column. M-B-01 builds on the entry skill (placement level): print that, do not invent a prerequisite.
- Earlier planner (19 to 20 Oct) differed: it had a guided-practice day and tens/ones on Day 11. Anything built on it is superseded.

For Days 2 and later: read the day's row and its Difficulty line first, then design.

## Grade 1 book (`Maths books\math_1 (1).pdf`)

Scanned, no text layer. **Printed page = pdf page minus 4.**

| Printed page | Content | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 2 to 3 | fingers and objects 1 to 9 | warm-up |
| 5 | Counting table (rows of pumpkins 6, doughnuts 8, oranges 9) | We Do, Check the Book |
| 6, 8 | count-and-write exercises | Days 4 to 5 (writing), not Day 1 |
| 8 to 10 | encircle, match, colour | suit Days 2 to 3 |
| 14 | Number ten (9 candies + 1) | I Do stage 3, make ten |
| 18 | Numbers 11 to 20 | I Do stage 4, past ten |

The book shows objects only up to 9. It has **no page that counts groups up to 20**, so the plan cites p.5 and p.18 and **says so** on the page. Do not claim the book covers 1 to 20 objects.

## Terminology

- "Ten Box" = ten-frame (2 rows of 5), used as a counting aid. Confirm wording with the programme team if they object.
- "Stones" are the manipulative; caps are an accepted substitute in the Prep Pack material list.
